Steps to Start a Computer

  1. Make sure the computer is plugged into a power source.
  2. Press the power button on the computer or laptop.
  3. Wait for the operating system to load.
  4. Enter your username and password on the login screen.
  5. The desktop appears — your computer is ready to use.
